About Diamond Training

Our Goal

Diamond Training is an online baseball equipment store created to help players improve their skills through effective practice and quality training equipment.

Baseball is a sport that requires practice, dedication, and the right equipment. Our goal is to make it easy for players, parents, and coaches to find equipment that can be used to improve different areas of the game.

Who We Serve

  • Youth baseball players
  • High school players
  • Baseball coaches
  • Parents
  • Players training at home

What We Offer

Our store offers batting tees, training baseballs, hitting nets, gloves, resistance bands, and other baseball practice equipment.
