Our Goal

Diamond Training is an online baseball equipment store created to help players improve their skills through purposeful practice and useful training equipment.

Baseball takes dedication and repetition. Our goal is to make it easier for players, parents, and coaches to find equipment for different areas of the game.

Who We Serve

  • Youth baseball players
  • High school players
  • Baseball coaches
  • Parents and families
  • Players training at home

What We Offer

Our store features batting tees, training baseballs, hitting nets, gloves, resistance bands, and other practice equipment.
